Our story

15 buildings. One company. One standard.

Africrest develops, owns and manages every estate entirely for rentals — for the modern South African.

Our above-industry-average lease renewal rates aren't a marketing line. They're the only number that matters in residential property, and they're the reason 9 out of 10 of our tenants would recommend us to a friend.

We focus on five fundamentals — security, value-for-money, service, facilities, location — and we deliver all five at every building, in every area we operate. From Sandton to Marshalltown, the standard doesn't change.

If your circumstances change mid-lease, you can move to any of our other estates penalty-free. We don't make money on your inconvenience.

Built for renters.
Not for sale.

Every Africrest building is purpose-built as a rental estate — not converted, not subdivided from a sold-off block. That changes everything.

Our facilities are designed to be used daily by 100+ residents, not staged for showings. Our buildings stay under one management — no body corporate politics, no absent landlords, no inconsistent maintenance.

And because we own every unit, our incentives match yours. Empty apartments cost us money. Unhappy tenants don't renew. Every business decision flows from those two facts.
