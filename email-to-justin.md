## Why this email body works

**Paragraph 1 — credibility frame.** Names the studio, names the SA-property stack expertise (Propficient, MRI, POPIA, B-BBEE — all real things Africrest deals with). Then states what was done ("we ran a full automated audit") and why he should care ("worth your fifteen minutes" — small ask).

**Paragraph 2 — the findings.** Three concrete data points (44, 28, 13+ domains). The phrase *"rather than send a written report, we built a working concept"* differentiates from every other agency that pitches with PDFs.

**Paragraph 3 — the two links.** Demo first, deck second. Both URLs short and shareable. Note the explicit *"both work on phone"* — Justin will read this on his phone, and you've removed the "I'll look later on my laptop" excuse.

**Paragraph 4 — the pricing.** Stated upfront. No surprises in the meeting. Plus the method-on-slide-9 line — that's the disclaimer-in-the-email version: "we're not bullshitting, the methodology is documented."

**Paragraph 5 — the two-path CTA.** Mirrors slide 11. Either reply for a meeting, or forward the links. Both move things forward without forcing him into a meeting before he's ready.

**Closing — short.** No "looking forward to hearing from you", no "feel free to reach out". Just sign off and stop. Confidence signal.

## What to do BEFORE sending

1. **Replace `[your number]`** with your actual mobile number
2. **Verify Justin's email address** — check LinkedIn or africrest.co.za contact page
3. **Verify the URLs work** — open both in incognito mode, click around, make sure nothing 404s
4. **Get the deck on a deck-only Vercel deployment** — currently the deck is one HTML file inside the demo folder. For email-as-pitch, you'd want it at `africrest-deck.vercel.app` separately so the URL is clean, OR you can keep it at `africrest-pitch.vercel.app/africrest-pitch-deck.html` and update the email accordingly
5. **Send from `tlotlisomosiana@phuturedigital.co.za`** — not Gmail. Domain matters for trust.
6. **Send Tuesday–Thursday, between 09:00–11:00 SAST**. Avoid Mondays (inbox triage) and Fridays (low engagement).

## What to do AFTER sending

- Wait **4 full working days** before any follow-up. The temptation to send "did you see my email" on day 2 is your worst enemy. Don't.
- If no reply by day 4: send the LinkedIn DM (separate file in this package — `outreach-package.md`).
- If no reply by day 7: one final email referencing the LinkedIn message.
- If no reply by day 14: drop it. Add to a 90-day re-engagement reminder.
