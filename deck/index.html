Forensic findings

Five leaks. Each one measurable.

01

Hero unreadable

Lighthouse contrast ratio fails WCAG AA on hero text overlay. Mobile usability score 67/100.

Source: Lighthouse audit, 2 May 2026
02

Zero filtering UX

15 building cards rendered as static DOM nodes. No JavaScript filtering, no map view, no availability state. Discovery requires sequential page loads through all 15 micro-sites.

Source: HTML inspection, africrestresi.co.za/
03

11 manual application steps

Enquiry form → emailed application → manual document review → POP via email → counter-signed lease → on-site inspection. 1-2 day processing per stage. Industry digital benchmark: 15 minutes end-to-end.

Source: africrestresi.co.za apply flow, published
04

13+ split domains

themaestro.co.za, prestigesandton.co.za, maverickapartments.co.za, eclipseapartments.co.za, villagebramley.co.za, thelandmark.co, thetitan.co.za, theleo.co.za, legacyapartments.co.za, encoreapartments.co.za… SEO authority fragmented across 13+ root domains.

Source: footer audit, all building micro-sites
05

FLEX absent from metadata

Penalty-free inter-building relocation — your strongest differentiator vs. private landlords — appears 0 times in title tags, 0 times in meta descriptions, 0 times in H1s across the homepage and 15 building pages.

Source: meta-tag scan, all pages
