About this estate

The Maestro is one of our newest developments — a luxury 86-unit estate on Sandton's prime 5th Street strip. Studio, 1 and 2-bedroom apartments are designed around a resort-style courtyard with pool, gym, business centre and rooftop entertainment areas.

Like every Africrest estate, The Maestro is built, owned and managed by us — our on-site team handles maintenance, security and resident services directly. Mid-lease moves to other Africrest buildings are penalty-free.

Apartment features

Designer kitchens with granite or quartz countertops. Built-in oven, stove, extractor. Prepaid electricity and water meters — only pay for what you use. Fitted space for washing machine. Spacious bathrooms with trendy fittings. Secure basement parking with access control.

Location

155 5th Street, Sandton — minutes from Sandton City, Nelson Mandela Square, Gautrain station and the M1 highway. Walking distance to restaurants, gyms, banks and grocers.
