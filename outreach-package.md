## 4. Objection-handling playbook

These are the five things Justin will most likely push back on. Prepare for each.

### "We already have an agency on retainer."

> "Understood. Most agencies do brand and visual design well but ship to Elementor or WordPress page-builders — which is exactly why your current site scored 44 on Performance. The work we're proposing is the engineering layer: Propficient integration, filtering UX, the per-unit cost-breakdown page, the application replacement. We can work alongside your incumbent agency on creative direction if that's the structure, but we'd own the build."

### "How do we know the conversion numbers?"

> "We instrument from day one. Plausible or PostHog, depending on your POPIA stance. We define three or four key metrics with you in week one — viewing requests, application starts, application completions — and you see the dashboard weekly. After 60 days of live data we sit down and look at lift versus the baseline. If the numbers aren't moving, that becomes the conversation. We're not selling hope; we're selling instrumentation."

### "What about Propficient? We have to integrate."

> "We've reviewed Propficient's API documentation. Phase 1 needs read access only — a daily availability sync via their export API. Phase 2 is the deeper integration: webhooks for application submissions, two-way state, POPIA-compliant audit log. We'd want a 30-minute call with Propficient's technical contact in week 1 of Phase 2 to confirm scope. If they push back, we'll know early and we'll re-scope Phase 2 transparently."

### "R95k is a lot."

> "Compared to a freelance template at R20k, sure. Compared to what an established agency would quote for the same scope — R200k to R500k in the SA market for corporate-tier custom builds — it's about half. We're priced as a starter studio with a working concept already shipped, not as a Sandton agency with a five-month discovery phase. Phase 1 is fixed-scope and milestone-billed, not time-and-materials, so the number doesn't drift. Two extra leases a month at your median listed rent of R6,890 recovers the entire Phase 1 in seven months — and that's the conservative scenario. Slide 3 of the deck has the full math with cited sources."

### "Send us a proposal."

> "Will do. Today I'll send a one-page Statement of Work with scope, timeline, milestones, and deliverables. Could we put fifteen minutes on Thursday for you and your marketing lead to walk through it together? If after fifteen minutes it's not the right fit, we shake hands and you keep the audit findings. Fair?"

## 6. Pre-meeting checklist

The day before the call:

- [ ] Demo URL loads cleanly in incognito (no cached files breaking the layout)
- [ ] Both your laptop AND your phone set up to mirror the demo. **Demo on phone, in his hands, is the close.** He has to feel it.
- [ ] Deck (`africrest-pitch-deck.html`) bookmarked, open in a second tab. Only switch to it if he asks "do you have a deck?"
- [ ] One-page SoW emailed the morning of, *before* the meeting
- [ ] Audit results page printed or open in a third tab — be ready to show the actual Lighthouse + AI scores
- [ ] If meeting at his office: arrive 15 minutes early. Sit in the lobby and observe how their actual prospects (not you) navigate the existing site on their phones. Mention what you saw in the first 30 seconds of the meeting.

**Speak as a principal of a studio, not a freelancer with a laptop.**

| Don't say | Say |
|-----------|-----|
| "I built this in five days" | "We built this concept" |
| "You'll work with me" | "You'll work with our principal team" |
| "I'm cheaper than agencies" | "We sit at the senior end of independent studios" |

| "Solo" | "Specialist" |

The Phuture Digital studio is real. It's you, plus the senior delivery network you bring in for engagements (designer, junior dev, content reviewer, QA). That's not lying — that's **how every solo principal launches a studio**. The week you sign Justin, you bring in the second body. By Phase 2 you have three. That's the normal trajectory; the framing just has to lead the operations, not lag them.

You've already won the hard part — you have a working artifact. The pitch from here is calm execution.
