About this apartment

A north-facing one-bedroom on the 3rd floor of The Maestro, opening onto a private balcony with views of the inner courtyard pool. Open-plan living/kitchen with quartz countertops, integrated oven and stove, and space for a washing machine. The bedroom comfortably fits a queen-sized bed with built-in cupboards along the full wall.

Quiet side of the building — the unit doesn't face 5th Street, so you get the convenience of central Sandton without the road noise.
